Personal Project

A small desktop application built with Electron that helps users make random decisions through an interactive interface.

I made the art using Aseprite and I wanted the app to be something cute and simple that I could send to my friends. I had a lot of fun designing the interface. I showed the prototype to my friends and added some quality-of-life changes based on their feedback, e.g., pressing Enter to insert an option, and not letting the user decide until they have inserted at least two options.

Figuring out how to make the buttons move and the cards stack over each other was the most challenging part of this project, but I'm very happy with the result!

You can fork the source code and run the app in your device or download it directly. Instructions on GitHub.

Technologies: JavaScript, Electron, HTML, CSS, Aseprite

Skills learned: UI/UX Design, pixelart and pixel animation, creative thinking, user-centered design

UBISS 2026 Project

A tree-robot that reacts to light and touch/vibration and lets you know exactly how it's feeling through its LCD screen.

This was a two-people group project made over a course of six-days for the UB Summer School. Making a functioning robot in six days seemed near impossible to me, especially with little to no prior experience. But we somehow got it done!

The body of the robot was made with laser-cutting and the stem by 3D- printing. An ESP32 kit was provided to us and every resource used was available in our FabLab. The criteria of the project was to build a robot where we must use four devices from the ESP32 kit that we picked by lottery. Ours were photoresistor, LCD module, vibration switch, and push button.

I sewed the fabric around the stem using the sewing machine in our lab, which was also a new experience for me. I crocheted the flowers and the leaves myself.

Technologies: ESP32, servo motor, laser cutter & 3d printer, sensors and electronics

Skills learned: embedded systems development, soldering, teamwork collaboration, problem-solving, hardware prototyping
